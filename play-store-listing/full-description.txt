Track attendance, plan classes and stay above your attendance target. ClassTrack is an attendance tracker and student planner with a bunk calculator, class timetable, homework reminders, exam countdowns and GPA tracking.

ATTENDANCE TRACKER AND BUNK CALCULATOR
- Mark each class present, absent or cancelled in one tap.
- See attendance percentage by subject and overall.
- Use the bunk calculator to see how many classes you can safely skip, or how many you need to attend to reach your target.
- Set your own attendance target, including 75%.
- Get attendance forecasts, safe-skip predictions and risk alerts before a class (Pro).

CLASS TIMETABLE AND SCHEDULE
- Build a weekly class timetable with day, week and month calendar views.
- Add rooms, professors, class links and one-off extra classes.
- Get class reminders and a nudge to record attendance after class.
- View today's classes and tasks from the home-screen widget.
- Sync your class schedule with Google Calendar (Pro).

AI TIMETABLE IMPORT
Take a photo of a timetable, upload a PDF or paste timetable text. ClassTrack's AI turns it into a weekly class schedule in seconds. Review it, make changes and save it - no need to type every class by hand.

HOMEWORK, ASSIGNMENTS AND EXAM PLANNER
- Keep a to-do list for homework, assignments and projects with due dates and reminders.
- See exam countdowns and keep upcoming deadlines in one student planner.
- Create an AI weekly study plan around your free time, exams and deadlines (Pro).
- Start the day with a summary of your classes, deadlines and exams (Pro).

AI STUDY ASSISTANT
Ask for a study plan, help breaking down a syllabus, a learning plan or study tips. The free plan includes 10 AI messages each month. Pro includes unlimited AI assistant messages and timetable imports.

GRADES AND GPA CALCULATOR
Log marks, track your GPA and see the score you need to reach your target.

NOTES, FLASHCARDS AND FOCUS
- Record study sessions with a focus timer.
- Build a daily study streak with the habit tracker.
- Track a student budget and expenses.
- Create formatted lecture notes, add images and PDFs, and make flashcards (Pro).

STUDENT OPPORTUNITIES
Find scholarships, internships, hackathons, competitions, free courses, certifications and student discounts. Free members see the latest picks. Pro includes the full library with search and filters.

EXPORT AND SHARE
Export attendance as PDF or CSV and export your timetable as a calendar (.ics) file (Pro). Share attendance and GPA milestones with friends.

PRIVATE AND SECURE
Sign in with Google or email to sync data across your devices. Your data is private to your account, and you can delete your account any time in Settings.

MADE FOR STUDENTS
ClassTrack is for college, university, engineering, medical, diploma, school and high-school students - and anyone who needs to manage attendance, a class timetable, homework, exams and grades.

CLASSTRACK PRO
Pro unlocks unlimited AI, AI study planning, smart notes and flashcards, attendance insights and predictions, Google Calendar sync, attendance risk alerts, daily agenda, data export, quiet hours and custom accent colours. Attendance tracking, the bunk calculator, timetable and to-do list are available on the free plan.
