ClassTrack is a study planner and attendance tracker in one app. Plan your study time, count down to exams, manage homework and assignments, follow your class timetable and always know your attendance percentage - with a bunk calculator that shows how many classes you can safely skip.

STUDY PLANNER AND EXAM PREP
- Add your exams and see a countdown to each one, so revision never starts too late.
- Plan homework, assignments and projects with due dates and reminders.
- Create an AI weekly study plan around your free time, exams and deadlines (Pro).
- Start each day with a summary of your classes, deadlines and exams (Pro).

ATTENDANCE TRACKER AND BUNK CALCULATOR
- Mark each class present, absent or cancelled in one tap.
- See attendance percentage by subject and overall.
- Use the bunk calculator to see how many classes you can skip, or how many you must attend to reach your target.
- Set your own attendance target, such as 75%.
- Get attendance forecasts, safe-skip predictions and risk alerts before class (Pro).

CLASS TIMETABLE AND SCHEDULE
- Build a weekly class timetable with day, week and month calendar views.
- Add rooms, teachers, class links and one-off extra classes.
- Get class reminders and a nudge to mark attendance after class.
- See today's classes and tasks on the home-screen widget.
- Sync your class schedule with Google Calendar (Pro).

AI TIMETABLE IMPORT
Take a photo of your timetable, upload a PDF or paste the text. ClassTrack's AI turns it into a weekly class schedule in seconds. Review, edit and save - no typing every class by hand.

AI STUDY ASSISTANT
Ask for an exam prep plan, a revision schedule, help breaking down a syllabus or study tips. The free plan includes 10 AI messages each month. Pro includes unlimited AI messages and timetable imports.

FOCUS TIMER AND STUDY HABITS
- Time your study sessions with the focus timer.
- Build a daily study streak with the habit tracker.
- Track your student budget and expenses.

NOTES AND FLASHCARDS
Write formatted lecture notes, add images and PDFs, and turn them into flashcards for revision (Pro).

GRADES AND GPA CALCULATOR
Log your marks, track your GPA and see the score you need to reach your target.

STUDENT OPPORTUNITIES
Find scholarships, internships, hackathons, competitions, free courses, certifications and student discounts. Free members see the latest picks. Pro unlocks the full library with search and filters.

EXPORT AND SHARE
Export attendance as PDF or CSV and your timetable as a calendar (.ics) file (Pro). Share attendance and GPA milestones with friends.

PRIVATE AND SECURE
Sign in with Google or email to sync across your devices. Your data is private to your account, and you can delete your account any time in Settings.

MADE FOR EVERY STUDENT
ClassTrack works for school, high-school, college, university, engineering, medical and diploma students - anyone who needs one place for a study planner, exam countdown, homework planner, class timetable, attendance and grades.

CLASSTRACK PRO
Pro unlocks unlimited AI, AI study planning, smart notes and flashcards, attendance insights and predictions, Google Calendar sync, attendance risk alerts, daily agenda, data export, quiet hours and custom accent colours. The study planner, exam countdowns, homework list, attendance tracker, bunk calculator and timetable are free.
